I use AI for growth, but I watch my use.

What I believe

Why wouldn't we use it?

I don't think technology is bad. A hammer can put a nail in the wall, and it can also break your head. So use it to the maximum of your benefit. Just don't be consumed by it.

There is no universal rule.

Someone using AI for work may be on it six or ten hours a day. Someone using it for fun, maybe five or fifteen minutes. What counts as too much is different for each of us.

Is it necessary?

When I open Instagram, I ask myself: is it necessary? Am I gaining anything out of it? I'm not saying that you stop doing it. I'm just saying that you should be aware.
